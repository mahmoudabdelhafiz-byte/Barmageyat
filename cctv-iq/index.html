Why CCTV-IQ

Traditional CCTV is excellent for reviewing what happened after an incident. CCTV-IQ is designed to add a computer-vision layer that continuously looks for configured events and brings the exceptions that matter to the attention of operations, security, or HSE teams.

✓Define monitored areas, safety rules, traffic conditions, and operational thresholds.
✓Use existing compatible camera feeds instead of replacing an entire CCTV estate solely to introduce analytics.
✓Surface events as actionable alerts with camera, location, time, and visual evidence.
✓Review recurring events and patterns to support safer and more efficient operations.

How CCTV-IQ works

How it works

The objective is not simply to detect objects. CCTV-IQ connects visual detection with configurable business rules so the right event can become a meaningful alert instead of another video stream someone must watch continuously.

01

Existing CCTV

Compatible cameras continue to provide live video feeds.

02

AI Analysis

Computer vision evaluates the scene for configured events.

03

Rules & Zones

Site rules determine what should trigger attention.

04

Proactive Alert

Relevant events are surfaced with context and evidence.

05

Review & Action

Teams acknowledge, investigate, act, and analyze trends.

Configurable use cases

Core capabilities

Use cases are configured around the camera position, scene, operating environment, and required response. Detection quality depends on camera view, lighting, calibration, video quality, and site conditions, so each deployment should be validated against the actual environment.

PPE

Missing PPE Detection

Identify people entering configured work areas without required visible PPE such as helmets or high-visibility vests, and raise an event for review.

ZONE

No-Entry & Restricted Zones

Monitor virtual zones or lines and alert when a person or vehicle enters an area that has been configured as restricted.

SPD

Vehicle Overspeed

Use calibrated camera views to identify vehicles that appear to exceed configured speed thresholds on monitored internal roads or operational lanes.

Requires site/camera calibration for meaningful speed estimation.
SMK

Visual Smoke / Fire Indicators

Detect visible smoke or flame-like conditions in monitored scenes and create an additional early-warning event for operations or safety teams.

Supplementary visual analytics — not a replacement for certified fire detection systems.
Q

Gate Congestion

Monitor queue build-up, lane occupancy, or vehicle counts at gates and approaches so teams can react before congestion materially affects operations.

WX

Rain, Sand & Low Visibility

Use camera imagery to flag severe rain, sand/dust conditions, or reduced visibility that may require operational attention or additional safety controls.

Important: CCTV-IQ is an AI-assisted monitoring layer. It supports — and does not replace — certified fire detection, access control, traffic enforcement, PPE procedures, human supervision, or other mandatory safety and security systems.

Frequently asked questions

Deployment

Do we need to replace our existing CCTV cameras?

The product is intended to add analytics to compatible existing video infrastructure where camera position, stream accessibility, image quality, lighting, and network conditions are suitable. Camera compatibility and performance should be assessed during deployment design.

Can different cameras have different AI rules?

Yes, the product concept supports configuring monitoring scenarios by area or camera—for example PPE rules in a workshop, congestion rules at a gate, and restricted-zone rules around sensitive areas.

Does CCTV-IQ replace our fire alarm or physical security systems?

No. CCTV-IQ is an additional AI-assisted visual monitoring layer. Certified fire detection, access control, safety procedures, and mandated security systems remain the primary controls.

Can it be used outside ports and terminals?

Yes. The same approach can apply to warehouses, logistics sites, industrial facilities, construction locations, campuses, and other environments where existing CCTV footage can support defined operational or safety use cases.
