Why AttendanceIQ

For distributed, operational, and multi-location teams, attendance should reflect the approved workplace—not simply whether someone can access a web form. AttendanceIQ uses the employee mobile experience and configured geofences to validate attendance actions at the right location.

✓Employees check in and out through the mobile application.
✓Configured geofences validate that the action occurs within an approved work zone.
✓Administrators review attendance activity centrally through the web application.
✓Attendance records can connect to workforce planning through API integration.

Designed for operational workforces

AttendanceIQ focuses specifically on mobile, location-aware attendance so it can be deployed alongside existing HR, payroll, or workforce planning systems without turning the attendance layer into a full HR suite.

How it works

How it works

A simple workflow keeps the process understandable for employees while preserving central visibility for administrators.

Open the mobile app

The employee signs in and opens the attendance action for the assigned work location.

Validate the work zone

AttendanceIQ checks the device location against the configured geofence for that site.

Record check-in or out

The attendance action is recorded with the relevant time and location validation result.

Review and integrate

Administrators review attendance centrally and connected systems can consume the attendance data through APIs.
