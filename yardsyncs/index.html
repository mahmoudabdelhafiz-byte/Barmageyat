Yardsyncs · by Barmageyat

Smarter truck appointments. Smoother terminal gates.

A configurable Truck Appointment System and modular Autogate platform built for container and multipurpose terminals—connecting trucking companies, drivers, terminal planners and gate operations.

Request a demonstrationExplore the platform
ONE CONNECTED TERMINAL WORKFLOW

From booking to gate-out

Plan terminal capacity, validate trips against the TOS, give drivers timely instructions and manage gate exceptions. Yardsyncs supports existing terminal gate systems through its integration API; Yardsyncs Autogate is an optional module.

Truck Appointment System

Self-service bookings, bulk uploads, import/export/empty and multipurpose cargo workflows, configurable slot capacity and live operational status.

Terminal Capacity & Control

Weekly and seasonal templates, category quotas, exception handling, slot closures, utilization insights and coordinated operations.

Modular Autogate

Lane device integration, QR and OCR-supported matching, gate decisions, exception handling, inspection workflows and optional edge resilience.

LIVE OPERATIONS

A clear view of appointment demand

See booked versus available capacity by hour and move type. Apply operational changes when conditions change, with documented reasons and visibility of overcapacity.

CONFIGURATION-FIRST

Capacity rules that fit your terminal

Configure weekly and seasonal scheduling plans, working hours, overbooking limits and move-type quotas without hard-coding a single customer's operation.
