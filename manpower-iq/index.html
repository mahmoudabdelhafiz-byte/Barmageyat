ManpowerIQ — Workforce Rostering & Manpower Planning

ManpowerIQ is a smart workforce rostering and manpower planning system designed to help companies plan, allocate, approve, and monitor manpower more efficiently across departments, shifts, and locations.
Built for ports, terminals, logistics operations, warehouses, industrial facilities, and shift-based businesses, ManpowerIQ replaces manual Excel-based planning with a controlled, digital, rule-based workforce management process.
Explore Capabilities
 

Why ManpowerIQ

Many companies still depend on manual spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages, and email approvals to manage rosters, overtime, leaves, and shift changes. This creates delays, errors, unfair allocation, and limited operational visibility.

ManpowerIQ provides one centralized platform to manage the full workforce planning cycle:


✔ Employee Master Data

✔ Skill & Certification Setup

✔ Shift Configuration

✔ Roster Planning

✔ Automatic Allocation

✔ Leave & Absence Management

✔ Approval Workflow

✔ Roster Publishing

✔ Dashboards & Reports

Core capabilities

Intelligent Rostering

Generate manpower rosters based on employee availability, department requirements, skills, shift rules, and operational demand.

Skill-Based Allocation

Assign employees only to positions they are qualified for based on skills, certifications, licenses, and training status.

Shift Configuration

Configure day shifts, night shifts, office shifts, training shifts, Ramadan schedules, weekend rules, and custom shift patterns.

Overtime Equalization

Track overtime and distribute it fairly across employees to reduce imbalance and improve cost control.

Leave & Absence Management

Manage annual leave, sick leave, emergency leave, absence records, and leave impact on roster coverage.

Approval Workflows

Support department-based approvals, manager approvals, delegation, escalation, and approval history.

Cross-Pooling

Allow qualified manpower to support other departments or locations during peak operations based on business rules.

Training Allocation

Plan training shifts and manage skill development without disrupting operational coverage.

Terminal / Location Rotation

Rotate employees across operational areas, terminal zones, equipment types, or departments to support fairness and capability growth.

Operational workforce planning

Built for operational workforce planning

ManpowerIQ is built for shift-based operations where roster quality affects safety, service levels, overtime, equipment coverage and day-to-day execution. It replaces fragmented spreadsheet planning with a controlled workforce-planning process that managers can review, approve and publish.

Demand-aware rostering

Build rosters around department requirements, operating shifts, workforce availability and the manpower needed to cover planned operations.

Skills & certification controls

Match people to assignments using skills, licenses, training status and qualification rules so critical positions are covered by eligible employees.

Fairer overtime & allocation

Track overtime and workforce distribution to reduce repeated over-allocation, improve fairness and give managers better visibility before publishing a roster.

Complete planning cycle

How it works

The platform brings together the planning controls normally spread across worksheets, emails and messaging groups.

Employee master data and organizational structure
Shift patterns and custom operational schedules
Leave, absence and roster coverage impact
Manager approvals, delegation and approval history
Cross-pooling across departments or locations
Training allocation without losing operational coverage
Terminal, area or location rotation planning
Dashboards, published rosters and reporting

Designed for operations

ManpowerIQ is particularly suited to ports, container terminals, logistics, warehouses and industrial environments where 24/7 coverage, qualifications and operating rules matter.

It can also be used by other organizations that manage complex shift-based workforces across multiple departments or locations.

Integration with AttendanceIQ

ManpowerIQ handles workforce planning and rostering. AttendanceIQ handles mobile, geofenced attendance execution. Together they can connect the planned roster with actual check-in and check-out data while keeping each product focused on its own job.

Plan

Define shifts, manpower requirements, eligible employees, leave, training and approvals in ManpowerIQ.

Execute

Employees check in and out through AttendanceIQ within configured work zones using the mobile attendance experience.

Compare & improve

Use planned and actual workforce data together to identify gaps, late attendance, uncovered positions and recurring staffing issues.
