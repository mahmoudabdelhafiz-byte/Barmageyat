CardIQ Identity by Barmageyat

CardIQ Identity — Corporate Digital Identity Control

CardIQ Identity is a corporate digital identity control platform that helps organizations issue, verify, update and revoke employee-facing professional identities from one controlled environment. It extends beyond digital business cards to address identity lifecycle, brand control, verification and offboarding.

Explore Capabilities
 

Why CardIQ Identity

CardIQ uses signature verification links, QR validation, company-controlled employee profiles, branded virtual meeting backgrounds with active employee QR, and admin review to help recipients confirm identity details and reduce risk.

Corporate digital identity control

Core capabilities

CardIQ Identity gives organizations a central way to issue, maintain, verify and revoke employee-facing digital identities. Instead of leaving business cards, public profiles and verification signals unmanaged, the company keeps control of the identity that represents its brand.

Issue and manage identities

Create company-controlled employee profiles and digital identity assets from a central administration layer, with consistent branding and ownership.

Verify before people trust

Give customers, partners and recipients clear ways to verify an organization or identity through controlled verification channels rather than relying on appearance alone.

Revoke when employment changes

Deactivate or revoke company identities when an employee leaves or changes role, reducing the risk of outdated profiles continuing to represent the organization.

Identity lifecycle

How it works

CardIQ Identity is designed around the full lifecycle of a professional identity, not only the moment a digital card is shared.

Company-controlled employee profiles and digital cards
Central updates to role, contact and brand information
Verification Hub for organization-level validation
Email-signature and QR-based verification workflows
Trusted-domain verification for official company domains
Controlled deactivation and offboarding
Administrative audit visibility and security controls
Directory-assisted lifecycle workflows for enterprise teams

Why this matters

An employee's professional identity can remain visible long after the underlying HR or directory record changes. CardIQ Identity gives IT, HR and corporate teams a dedicated control point for the identity presented to the outside world.

It complements internal identity systems by focusing on public-facing professional identity, verification and brand control.
